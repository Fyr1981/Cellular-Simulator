Executes the photosynthesis command to generate energy.

Adds a fixed amount of energy (20 units) to the executing cell. This simulates photosynthesis or other energy generation mechanisms in the cellular automaton. The energy is added directly to the cell's current energy reserves without any preconditions.

Parameters
SimReference to the simulator environment (unused)
AgentReference to the cell executing this command
Note
Always adds exactly 20 energy units
No maximum energy limit is enforced by this command
Represents a basic energy production mechanism
