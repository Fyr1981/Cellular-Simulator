Executes the turn left command to rotate the cell counterclockwise.

Rotates the cell's facing direction 90 degrees to the left (counterclockwise). This changes the cell's orientation which affects the direction for subsequent movement, eating, and division commands. The rotation is instantaneous and does not consume energy or require any preconditions.

Parameters
SimReference to the simulator environment (unused)
AgentReference to the cell executing this command
Note
Rotation is always exactly 90 degrees counterclockwise
No energy cost is associated with turning
Affects the forward direction for other directional commands
